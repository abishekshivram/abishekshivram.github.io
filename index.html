4 Description About

I design arithmetic-heavy digital hardware at Texas Instruments: the DSP and calibration datapaths inside high-speed RF data converters, taken from architecture and RTL through bit-accurate modeling, verification, and post-silicon validation.

I also build tools that automate this work. dp_gen, my Python-to-SystemVerilog compiler, fuses arithmetic chains into carry-save trees and generates bit-exact golden models for verification. My IIT Madras thesis was on in-memory compute, and I've taught computer architecture and RISC-V to 150+ undergraduates.

7 Detailed Description Experience

7.1 Texas Instruments: Digital Design Engineer, High-Speed Converters

Bengaluru, IndiaJul 2022 – Present

7.1.1 Datapath Compiler

Architected and sole-authored dp_gen, a Python-to-SystemVerilog compiler that infers bit-widths, fuses arithmetic chains into truncated CSA trees across multi-phase clocks, and auto-derives bit-exact golden Python models for cocotb equivalence checking, collapsing days of hand-written DSP RTL into seconds.

7.1.2 DSP Architecture

Architected and implemented RTL for high-performance DSP modules, including digital down- and up-converters (DDC/DUC) for high-speed AFEs, with a novel hitless rate-switching mechanism that maintains absolute phase determinism relative to SYSREF.

7.1.3 Modeling and Verification

Developed Python bit-accurate models for SNR/SFDR analysis and used them to set up cocotb verification regressions. Ran synthesis-driven design-space exploration (PPA) to optimize retiming and pipelining.

7.1.4 Calibration and Error Correction

Built and verified the digital error correction and background calibration subsystems for high-speed pipelined RF ADCs, driving closure across analog, mixed-signal verification, firmware, and lab teams. Hardened a space-grade variant for radiation tolerance with SEU-resilient calibration registers.

7.1.5 Post-Silicon Validation

Led post-silicon validation for these modules, writing test cases that cover JESD protocols and devising black-box debug strategies to resolve functional issues with limited visibility.

7.1.6 Mentoring and Teaching

Mentored an intern building an agentic AI framework for DSP RTL development, covering spec-to-RTL generation, testcase generation with self-simulation, and lint fixes.

Designed and delivered workshops and lectures on computer architecture and RISC-V for 150+ sophomores over three years in the WISH program, including lab exercises that guide students to implement a RISC-V core in a week.
