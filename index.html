About

I design arithmetic-heavy digital hardware at Texas Instruments: the DSP and calibration datapaths inside high-speed RF data converters, taken from architecture and RTL through bit-accurate modeling, verification, and post-silicon validation. I also build tools that automate this work. dp_gen, my Python-to-SystemVerilog compiler, fuses arithmetic chains into carry-save trees and generates bit-exact golden models for verification. My IIT Madras thesis was on in-memory compute, and I've taught computer architecture and RISC-V to 150+ undergraduates.
